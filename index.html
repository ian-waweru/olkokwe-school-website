About Olkokwe

Preparing Learners For Tomorrow.

Olkokwe Senior School is committed to providing an environment where learners can develop academically, socially and personally.

Through quality teaching, discipline, co-curricular activities and a culture of responsibility, we seek to equip learners with knowledge, skills and values that prepare them for life beyond school.

Our Vision

To nurture learners who are equipped to make a positive contribution to society.

Our Mission

To provide quality education while developing discipline, character, talent and innovation.

From the Principal

A Message From Our Principal.

At Olkokwe Senior School, we believe that education is not simply about academic achievement. It is about developing responsible, disciplined and confident young people who are prepared to make a meaningful contribution to society.

We are committed to creating an environment in which every learner has the opportunity to learn, grow, discover their talents and develop the character needed to face the future with confidence.

Mr. Ngetich Kipsang Bonde Principal, Olkokwe Senior School
